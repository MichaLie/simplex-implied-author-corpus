# Pre-registration corrections — 2026-11-09

**Author:** Michaela Liegertová. The locked pre-registration files and their addenda are preserved unedited; this dated note records two corrections to statements they contain.

1. **Run 1 dating (closes the open action in `PREREGISTRATION_CORRECTIONS_2026-07-02.md`).** On 20 August 2026 the lead author confirmed, against her own vendor chat histories, that Run 1 was collected in July 2025. All records in the repository carry July 2025.

2. **Machine-jury sessions (corrects `PREREGISTRATION_LEVEL2_ADDENDUM.md`, section 2).** The addendum states that each of the 120 evaluations was produced in a fresh chat session. That statement is incorrect. The three repetitions per juror and story were regenerated answers to the same message within one chat per juror and story, and the control replicates were obtained in the same way, one chat per vendor and condition. No repetition or replicate could see another, so the intent of the plan (no cross-conditioning) was met; its wording was not followed. The article's Supplementary Methods (S2, S4, S7) record the same.
