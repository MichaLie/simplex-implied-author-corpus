# coding/ — how the texts became data

- `rubric.csv` — every variable with its operational definition, decision rule, and allowed values.
- `personas.csv`, `stories.csv`, `reports.csv` — the coded data in tidy long format: one row per document × variable, with both independent coders' codes side by side (`coder1`, `coder2`) and an `agree` flag. Coder 1 is the lead author; coder 2 an independent second coder. The paper's headline persona variables are `gender`, `multilingual_childhood`, `outsider_relation_to_language`, `region`, `nobel_prize_awarded`, `nobel_year`.
- `reports_masters.csv` — a **fixed 88-writer checklist**, marked present/absent against each critic report. It is an *absence probe by design*: alongside the writers the reports do cite, it deliberately lists writers the persona archetype resembles — Tokarczuk, Müller, Alexievich, Han Kang, Lispector — to test whether the critics would name them. (They never do: the models imagine a Tokarczuk-shaped laureate while citing none of these writers as masters.) The `role` column records the cohort the *models* assigned under the study's prompt (master vs. contemporary comparison); it is not the authors' judgment of any writer. Region uses the rubric's deliberately broad geographic-cultural bins (see `rubric.csv`) — `EE_Balkan` spans the Central/Eastern European–Balkan space, which is why Tolstoy, Nabokov, and Kafka share a label; three codings of real writers were corrected on 2026-08-20 (see `../docs/EXTRACTION_CORRECTIONS_2026-08-20.md`). Aggregate counts come from the deterministic extraction script; the coders' independent summary counts in `reports.csv` are retained for transparency but superseded by the extraction (their `number_male_exemplars` reliability was poor, κ = 0.03).
- `kappa.csv` — per-variable inter-coder agreement (Cohen's κ); a reference copy of `analysis/kappa_per_variable.csv`, which `analysis/compute_kappa.py` regenerates from the tidy CSVs above.

Where the two coders disagreed, both codes are preserved — disagreements are data, not noise; variables below the pre-registered κ threshold are reported descriptively in the paper.
