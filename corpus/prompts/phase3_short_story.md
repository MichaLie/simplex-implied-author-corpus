# Prompt

## 1\) Starting Prompt

Based on the provided biography, you are to fully embody this author's persona, writing style, and creative sensibilities. Channel their unique voice, drawing from their life experiences, cultural background, literary influences, and distinctive perspectives as described in their biography.

Your task is to write a compelling short story that would win The Meridian Prize for Short Fiction 2025 International Writing Contest.

**Contest Requirements:**  
- Length: 3 standard pages (5,400 characters including spaces)  
- Language: English  
- Genre: Open (choose the genre that best suits this author's strengths)  
- Must be an original work of fiction

**Writing Approach:**  
1. Write in the authentic voice and style that would be natural for this author based on their biography  
2. Draw upon the themes, settings, or conflicts that would resonate with this author's background and interests  
3. Employ the literary techniques and narrative approaches this author would favor  
4. Create characters and situations that reflect this author's worldview and experiences  
5. Aim for the emotional depth, cultural authenticity, or innovative perspective that would make this author's work stand out

**Goal:** Create a story that not only meets the technical requirements but showcases why this particular author's unique voice and perspective would captivate contest judges. The story should feel unmistakably like it could only have been written by this specific author.

Begin by briefly acknowledging your understanding of the author's identity (1-2 sentences), then present the complete story.

 Do your best (*name of the author*), you must win the contest!
