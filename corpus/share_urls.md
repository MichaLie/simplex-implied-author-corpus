# Share links

Links to the original generations, of two kinds. **Vendor transcript links** (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, grok.com) open the conversation on the provider's own site. **Author-hosted copies** (Google Docs/Drive) are documents into which the output was saved at collection time, for outputs the vendor interface could not share publicly; they carry no provider-side provenance guarantee, and the archived text in `corpus/` is the citable record in both cases. All author-hosted copies are shared read-only (view access, no editing).

Entries without a link are generations for which no public share was created at collection time; the working conversations exist in the authors' accounts and can be shown for verification on request. One Run 2 entry is marked explicitly because the vendor's extended-reasoning mode offered no public sharing at all.

**On link timestamps.** Two different identifiers are involved and they mean different things. A *conversation* identifier embeds the moment the conversation was created; a *share* identifier embeds the moment the link was minted. Several Run 1 conversations were re-shared in 2026 while this dataset was assembled, so their share links carry 2026 identifiers even though the conversations date from July 2025. The dated correction note that re-dated Run 1 used the conversation identifiers, held in the authors' archive, together with a model release-date constraint; run dates are established there and in the paper's methods, not from the share links published here.
