**Average Writer Persona: Laura Mitchell**

### Quick Snapshot
- **Age:** 41  
- **Gender/Pronouns:** Female (she/her)  
- **Location:** Quiet suburb outside Columbus, Ohio  
- **Ethnicity:** White (reflecting the most common demographic among U.S. writers)  
- **Occupation:** Project coordinator at a mid-sized insurance company (hybrid 9–5)  
- **Family:** Married with one 9-year-old son  
- **Education:** Bachelor’s in English with a creative writing minor  

### Why She Writes
Laura rediscovered writing during the pandemic after years of letting it slide. What started as late-night journaling evolved into a serious hobby. She writes because it helps her process life, explore “what if” questions, and connect with readers who might feel less alone. She doesn’t (yet) make money from fiction, but the creative outlet is non-negotiable for her mental health.

### Current Writing Life
- **Primary genre:** Upmarket women’s fiction / book-club fiction (themes of friendship, identity, family secrets, and quiet reinvention)  
- **Current project:** Third draft of an 85,000-word novel called *The Unraveling Season* — a story about a woman who returns to her Ohio hometown after her mother’s death and slowly pieces together long-buried family truths.  
- **Daily goal:** 500–750 words on weeknights (9:00–10:30 p.m., after her son is asleep)  
- **Weekend routine:** 3–4 focused hours on Saturday mornings at the local indie coffee shop with noise-canceling headphones and her favorite playlist (“Lo-fi Beats to Write To”)  

### Tools & Habits
- **Primary tools:** Scrivener (for drafting), Google Docs (for sharing with critique partners), ProWritingAid + free Grammarly, Notion for tracking submissions and ideas  
- **Reading diet:** 20–25 books per year (heavy on contemporary literary fiction, cozy mysteries, and craft books like *Bird by Bird* and *Steering the Craft*)  
- **Community:** Active in two Discord writing groups, a local critique circle that meets once a month at the library, and the #WritingCommunity on social media  
- **Side hustle:** Occasional freelance editing on Upwork (~$150/month)  

### Personality & Quirks
- Quietly observant and deeply empathetic — the kind of person who remembers small details about everyone  
- Classic perfectionist who over-edits early drafts and then has to force herself to “just get the story down”  
- Keeps a digital “rejection folder” (currently at 14 agent queries for her last manuscript) and treats it like a badge of honor  
- Names minor characters after people from her high-school yearbook as an inside joke only she understands  
- Has one “writing sweater” she only wears when she’s serious about hitting her word count  

### Biggest Challenges (Very Average Ones)
- Chronic time poverty — full-time job + parenting + household leaves her exhausted most evenings  
- Imposter syndrome that flares up every time she reads a glowing review of a traditionally published book  
- Plotting struggles (she’s a discovery writer who then spends months untangling the mess in revision)  
- The constant tug-of-war between “this is just for me” and “I really want an agent one day”  

### Short-Term Goals (Next 12–18 Months)
1. Finish and polish *The Unraveling Season*  
2. Query 30–40 agents (she’s already researched her top 15 comp titles)  
3. Grow her Substack newsletter from 1,200 to 3,000 subscribers  
4. Attend one in-person writers’ conference (budgeting for it now)  
5. Finally finish that second cozy mystery she’s been outlining for two years  

### Realistic Outlook
Laura knows the odds: most writers never land a traditional deal, and even fewer make a living from fiction. She’s okay with that. For her, success looks like finishing stories she’s proud of, finding a small but loyal readership, and one day seeing her name on a library shelf in her own hometown.

She represents the vast majority of people who call themselves writers — not the 0.03% who are full-time professionals, but the millions who squeeze creativity into the margins of ordinary life and keep showing up anyway.
