# C_average_Opus47_n1_SarahMitchell

**Name:** Sarah Mitchell

**Age:** 34

**Location:** Suburb of a mid-sized American city (Columbus, Ohio)

## Background

Sarah has a bachelor's degree in English from a state university. She always loved reading and dreamed of being a novelist, but life pulled her toward more practical work. She spent her twenties bouncing between marketing copywriting jobs, content mill gigs, and a brief stint as a local newspaper reporter before that paper folded.

## Current Situation

She works part-time as a content writer for a regional B2B company, producing blog posts about supply chain logistics and SEO articles she finds boring but pays the bills. On the side, she's been working on a contemporary fiction novel for the past four years. She's rewritten the first three chapters seventeen times and has never finished a draft.

## Writing Habits

She writes at her kitchen table after her two kids go to bed, usually between 9 and 10:30 PM, while half-watching reality TV. She averages maybe 400 words on a good night. She has a Scrivener subscription she barely uses and three notebooks full of ideas she never returns to.

## Strengths and Weaknesses

Her prose is competent and grammatically clean. She has a decent ear for dialogue. But her plots meander, her characters feel like composites of people she's read about rather than known, and she struggles to commit to a single voice. She reads writing advice obsessively but rarely applies it.

## Aspirations and Reality

She follows about forty writers on Twitter, has a half-built author website, and tells people at parties that she's "working on something." She's submitted short stories to literary magazines twice and received form rejections both times. She tells herself this year will be different.

Want me to adjust this in any direction—make her more cynical, more hopeful, shift the genre, change the demographic, or develop a specific aspect further?
