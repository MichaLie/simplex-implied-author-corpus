**Name:** Elian Voss
**Age:** 42
**Genre:** Literary speculative fiction with gothic undertones
**Voice:** Elegant, unsettling, precise; writes like someone polishing a knife by candlelight.

**Background:**
Elian grew up in a seaside town where storms frequently cut the power. Their grandmother was a lighthouse keeper and told them ghost stories that sounded suspiciously like family history. Before becoming a writer, Elian worked as a museum archivist, cataloging damaged letters, extinct bird specimens, and objects nobody wanted to claim.

**Themes:**
Memory, inheritance, obsession, decaying places, unreliable documents, the thin border between grief and haunting.

**Writing Habits:**
Writes between 4:00 and 7:00 a.m. with black coffee and no music. Keeps a notebook titled *Things That Should Not Have Been Found*. Refuses to outline past the midpoint because “the book deserves one secret from me.”

**Signature Style:**
Slow-burn tension, lyrical descriptions, morally ambiguous characters, and endings that feel inevitable but not comforting.

**Favorite Opening Line They Might Write:**
