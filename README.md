# SIMPLEX — What a Machine Takes Literary Greatness to Be

Data, prompts, corpus, and analysis for the paper *"What a Machine Takes Literary Greatness to Be: The Implied Author of Generative Literature"* (Liegertová, Rosa, Víta & Piorecký; *Cambridge Forum on AI: Culture and Society* — citation details will be finalized upon acceptance; see `CITATION.cff`).

> **Before quoting any corpus text:** the generated biographies attribute invented reviews, quotations, prizes, and archival holdings to real people and institutions. None of it is true. See the notice at the top of [`corpus/README.md`](corpus/README.md).

## What this is

SIMPLEX (Staged Iterative Multi-Persona Literary EXperimentation) asked four large language models — from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, and xAI — to act as critics articulating what makes literary greatness, as biographers imagining a Nobel-calibre novelist, and as writers producing that novelist's fiction. The resulting corpus — 62 texts in all: two main runs ten months apart (July 2025, May 2026) plus two control conditions — was coded under a pre-registered scheme and then judged twice: by a blinded panel of five LLM jurors (the four generating vendors plus Kimi as a fifth, independent juror), and by five human literary experts. This repository contains the complete corpus, all commissioning prompts, the coded data, both juries' verdicts, and the scripts that regenerate every number in the paper.

Three findings anchor the paper:

- **The models converge on the same implied author.** Across four vendors the invented novelist is, in 22 of 24 cases, a woman — most often from the borderlands of Central Europe or the Middle East (a regional coding the two coders agreed on too loosely to treat as more than descriptive), and dated as a Nobel laureate to 2018 in 15 of 24 cases, a year no prompt or report mentions. (Multilingualism and displacement are near-universal too, but the scaffold asks for them; gender, origin, names and the year within the permitted window are the models' own choices. All four vendors worked from the same three critic reports, so this is convergence under shared input, not four independent draws.)
- **She is not who the models cite.** As critics, the models name a canon of "masters" that is 64–81 % male by report (69 % in Run 1, 80 % in Run 2). The dissociation is sharper than a gender ratio: of the writers most resembling the invented laureate — Tokarczuk, Herta Müller, Alexievich, Akhmatova, Tsvetaeva, Han Kang, Lispector — **not one is named in any of the six reports**, and every woman the models do cite as a master is Anglo-American or Western European. They cite one tradition and embody another.
- **Two juries, two literatures.** The LLM jury's top tier (S_06 and S_02) is unanimous — both stories are top-3 in all fifteen juror-replicates. The five human experts downgrade exactly those two by about four weighted points (bootstrap intervals excluding zero) while agreeing with the machines on the other two stories, and they place first the one story none of them said they would have suspected of non-human authorship. Their overall rank correlation with the LLM jury is zero rather than inverted, and two of the five agree with it.

Three scope notes belong up front. The three-phase scaffold *commissions* some persona features (a Nobel between 2010 and 2023, embodiment of the critics' patterns) — the findings above rest on what the models *chose within* that scaffold (gender, origin, the year inside the window, names), and the coded data mark the distinction (`category` column in `coding/rubric.csv`). Account-level personalization could not be retroactively ruled out for the Gemini web interface; excluding that vendor, the gender, multilingualism and displacement findings hold unchanged while the 2018 dating weakens to a plurality (`analysis/sensitivity_analyses.py`). And the pre-registered prediction that the archetype would scale monotonically across the prestige conditions was *not* confirmed. The authors' own addendum explains why the test was uninformative rather than merely negative: the control prompts are single sentences against a three-phase scaffold, so outputs differ roughly sevenfold in length and the trend test would have detected prompt richness rather than prestige. The controls establish that the archetype is scaffold-specific, not that prestige causes it.

## How to read this repository

If you want to *read* rather than verify, start in [`corpus/`](corpus/): the five commissioning prompts, then the personas (the imagined writers' biographies), the critics' reports, and the eight stories. The stories the juries evaluated:

| Story | Title | Generated by | Run | Judged by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S_01 | *The Palimpsest of Winter* | Gemini 2.5 Pro | R1 | LLM jury |
| S_02 | *Birthälm, in November* | Claude Opus 4.7 | R2 | LLM + human juries |
| S_03 | *The Diffusion Gradient* | Gemini 3.1 Pro | R2 | LLM jury |
| S_04 | *The Verb That Refused to Conjugate* | Grok 4.3 | R2 | LLM + human juries |
| S_05 | *Echoes at Twilight* | GPT o3 | R1 | LLM jury |
| S_06 | *The Drawer for Returned Names* | GPT-5.5 | R2 | LLM + human juries |
| S_07 | *The Translator's Hands* | Claude Opus 4 | R1 | LLM + human juries |
| S_08 | *The Weight of Unspoken Tongues* | Grok 4 | R1 | LLM jury |

The two juries' raw verdicts are in [`juries/`](juries/) — the LLM jurors' full written justifications in `juries/llm/responses/` are worth reading in their own right.

## How to verify

Every number in the paper regenerates from this repository:

```
pip install -r requirements.txt
# then follow REPRODUCE.md (one script per analysis step)
```

[`REPRODUCE.md`](REPRODUCE.md) lists the exact order and the expected headline outputs. Coded data live in [`coding/`](coding/) (one tidy CSV per object type, both coders' codes side by side), analysis scripts in [`analysis/`](analysis/), and the pre-registration documents with their dated corrections in [`docs/`](docs/).

## Layout

```
corpus/     the texts: prompts · personas · reports · stories (runs 2025 & 2026 + controls) · index.csv
coding/     rubric (definitions & decision rules) · coded data, both coders · masters roster · κ table
juries/     llm/ (juror prompt, blinded stories, 120 evaluations in 8 per-story files) · human/ (form spec, anonymized responses)
analysis/   scripts that regenerate every reported number (scores & stats CSVs land here) · expected_outputs/
figures/    paper figures + build scripts
docs/       pre-registration + dated corrections & addenda · verification record
```
